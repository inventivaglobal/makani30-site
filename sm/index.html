01
The campaign in a nutshell

Thirty years is the story. Let's relive the journey.

The anniversary is evidence of consistency, care, trust and stewardship. The campaign makes longevity tangible through the land, the herd, real people, the work before dawn and the promise carried across Australia.

02
The creative spine

One story, seven connected chapters.

Every piece of campaign content should strengthen at least one chapter. Together they move from the promise made to the promise continuing.

01

The promise

Consistency before spectacle.

Show the discipline behind a promise kept: quiet routines, repeatable standards and care that earns trust long before the finished product is seen.

02

The land

Country as living context.

Let country carry scale and atmosphere. Dawn, weather, pasture and texture ground the campaign in a distinctly Australian visual world.

03

The herd

Care shown with dignity.

Portraits and working moments reveal attentive husbandry, calm handling and the living responsibility behind every standard.

04

The people

Real faces and voices.

Paul and the Makani team bring warmth and memory through real faces, real voices and the knowledge carried in everyday work.

05

The journey

The promise, kept moving.

Vehicles, cold rooms, loading bays and depot rhythms turn national reach into a visible journey from source to customer.

06

The milestone

Thirty years, made tangible.

Archive details, anniversary rituals and then-and-now contrasts make thirty years feel immediate, specific and worth celebrating.

07

The next thirty

The promise continues.

End on forward motion: the same values carried into new seasons, new relationships and the work still ahead.

The face behind the promise.

Paul's own words, real footage and verified archive remain the source of truth. Our reference-led concept work establishes a coherent visual direction before the final shoot.

ICS in-house character and visual development model

Identity continuity before the camera rolls.

Anima AI is ICS's in-house identity-led character and visual development system. Built around client-supplied reference photography, it helps preserve recognisable facial structure, age, expression and wardrobe continuity while exploring controlled campaign environments. These frames are AI-generated production concepts, not documentary photographs.
