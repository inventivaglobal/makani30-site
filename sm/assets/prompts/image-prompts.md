# SM30 AI Image Prompt Pack

These prompts cover brand atmosphere only. Real people, real Makani locations,
history, certification and documentary events require genuine client material.

## Shared visual direction

Photorealistic Australian documentary photography, quiet and premium, warm but
not orange, muted earth tones, natural film grain, 35 mm photographic feeling,
soft atmospheric depth, unstaged composition and physically believable light.
Cattle are living animals shown calmly and with dignity.

## Shared negative prompt

No text, captions, watermarks, logos, labels, readable number plates, food
styling, meat products, cuts of meat, slaughter imagery, processing floor,
distressed animals, deformed cattle, duplicate animals, extra limbs, unreal
anatomy, identifiable people, corporate stock-photo posing, glossy advertising
lighting, generic American ranch visual language, cowboy imagery, barns or
fences that read as American, oversaturated orange grading, teal-and-orange
grading, artificial HDR, plastic skin or fur, surreal haze, fake archive
damage, fake certification marks or invented Makani branding.

Text and identity will be overlaid later in HTML/CSS.
