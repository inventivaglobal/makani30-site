OUR STORY

Three decades in the trade. One promise, kept.

What began in 1996 with a handful of loyal clients has grown into one of Australia's leading meat wholesalers, supplying major supermarket chains, independent butchers, top-end restaurants and corporate caterers right across the country.

Thirty years on, the promise hasn't changed: prime quality Australian meat, honestly priced, delivered in perfect condition. Every time.

1996
Makani Meats is founded, a 100% Australian-owned wholesaler with a simple vision: Australia's finest meat, at honest prices.
2000s
Vertical integration with Grange Foods, Makani now purchases, processes, warehouses and distributes its own meat, with complete control over quality and price.
2010s
Stringent quality control earns accreditation access to export markets in the United States, Japan and Korea.
Today
Depots in Sydney, Newcastle, Brisbane and Perth service a wide range of clients nationally, backed by an AUS-MEAT A+ rated processing plant.
2026
Thirty years in the trade, and we're only warming up.
